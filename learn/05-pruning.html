What pruning experts does

Pruning keeps the K most important experts in every layer and deletes the rest. Mechanically it is just slicing three tensors. The quality cost comes from tokens whose favourite experts are gone.

Rows with numbers are measured on Qwen3.5-35B-A3B in this project (REAP criterion, held-out eval set). Params = 2.43 B always-on + 0.45 B vision + K x 126 M. "Routing mass kept" is the share of all top-8 picks in the calibration set that land on a surviving expert (analyze_stats.py).

What to remember

For each layer, 04_prune.py keeps the K slices of experts.gate_up_proj and experts.down_proj and the K matching rows of the router matrix gate.weight, and writes "num_experts": K into config.json. Nothing else changes.

The router still works, because softmax and top-8 simply run over fewer candidates. But a token whose best experts were deleted is now served by its 9th, 10th, 11th choice, experts trained for slightly different tokens. The more of the routing that has to fall back like this, the worse the model gets: 65% routing mass kept at K=128 was fine, 28% at K=48 broke the model.

Active parameters per token do not change (still 8 experts), so the pruned model runs at about the same speed but needs much less memory.
