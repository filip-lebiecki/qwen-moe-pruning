What to remember

Read the name: the number is roughly the bits per weight; K = k-quant (blocks inside super-blocks of 256 with their own quantized scales); I = i-quant (non-uniform levels or lattice codebooks, needs an importance matrix); _S / _M / _L = small, medium, large mix (how many sensitive tensors get extra bits); XXS…XS = the smallest variants of an i-quant.

Measure in three ways: KL divergence to the full-precision model is the most sensitive (it sees every token's whole distribution); perplexity shows the effect on predicting text; benchmarks show whether tasks still get solved. Small KL changes may never reach a benchmark; a large one always does.

Choose by fit first. A file that fits in VRAM with room for context is many times faster than one that spills into system RAM. Within what fits, pick the largest type you can, and compare models of different sizes at the same file size, not at the same bit count.
